# Governance

**Summary**: How the HELP partnership is structured and governed — who decides, where the money moves, and the search for a new regional partnership structure to sit above or alongside HELP/WOB and HWC.
**Last updated**: 10-05-2026.

---

- *HELP next structure approach* (Email from Tim, 2026-09-04): The four-part path forward for the regional framework — lean on state funding to drive work at scale, since state programs are starting to want certain qualities; take a hard look at the strengths and weaknesses of both HELP/WOB and HWC; and build the thing that does not exist yet, an "UMWRA+" model: a JPA plus a strong program manager holding the master agreement with USFS, so the partnership keeps the WOB/HELP capacity while shoring up its gaps. Proposed members run to three counties (Eldorado, Placer, Sacramento), water agencies (EDWA, PCWA), utilities (Sac Sewer), RCDs, irrigation districts and groundwater authorities. IFNF funding through the end of 2026 would pay for a workshop to work it out. Related: [[WOB]] · [[Charter]] · [[Baseline]]. Keywords: governance, JPA, UMWRA, HWC, state funding, program manager
