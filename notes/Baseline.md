# Baseline

**Summary**: The origins, history and foundational documents behind the HELP charter process, and the baseline conditions it is built against.
**Last updated**: 10-05-2026.

---

- *Healthy Eldorado Landscape Partnership 10-Year Plan* (v1, 2023-05-01): The founding HELP strategy. Sets landscape-scale outcomes against TCSI's Ten Pillars of Resilience and crosswalks them to postfire recovery, wildfire risk, slope stability, water and sediment retention, wood processing and aquatic connectivity; names the pinch points (agency and contractor capacity, scarce biomass utilization, project scale, funding and cash flow, public sentiment); and lays out the phased plan — Strategy, Data Management, Building the Project List, Implementation — with the Executive Committee partners (ENF, American Forests, Blue Forest, Great Basin Institute, NFWF, Sierra Nevada Conservancy, American Rivers).
